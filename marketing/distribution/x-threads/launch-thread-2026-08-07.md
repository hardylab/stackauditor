1/ We just shipped StackAuditor.

Upload a screenshot of your SaaS stack and get a 1-page plain-language audit — wasted spend, redundant seats, unused features, top security red flags — in under 60 seconds.

2/ The reason we built it:

The average 5-person agency is on 27 SaaS tools. Nobody has time to audit them. Owners find out they have 3 CRMs and 14 seats of unused software when the renewal lands.

3/ What it does:
- Instant screenshot-to-audit — drop a PNG or PDF of any SaaS dashboard, structured 1-page report back in under 60s, no setup.
- Plain-language findings — every flagged item comes with a one-sentence "why it matters" and a one-click action, no jargon.
- Email-delivered PDF — the audit lands in your inbox as a clean PDF you can forward to a client or co-founder.

4/ What it does NOT do:
- Screenshot OCR is best-effort — very small fonts, custom dashboards, or non-English UIs may be missed. We flag low-confidence reads and ask you to label tools manually.
- It is informational, not advice. Financial and security findings are guidance, not a substitute for a human auditor, lawyer, or accountant.

5/ Demo + free audit: https://hardylab.github.io/stackauditor/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=x-thread&utm_campaign=stackauditor&utm_content=launch-thread-2026-08-07

6/ Who it is for: solo consultants, fractional operators, and 1–5 person agencies running 5+ SaaS tools.
Who it is NOT for: enterprises with procurement cycles, or anyone who wants a "negotiate my contracts" service.

7/ What is broken / missing / next:
- Vision model accuracy on dense custom dashboards is the open problem we are chasing this week.
- Pro tier (monthly re-audit + alerts) ships in ~2 weeks. Waitlist is open.

8/ First audit is free, no card. If you try it, tell me what is missing. Replies > likes.
